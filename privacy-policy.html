Privacy Policy

Introduction

Heart Scribed ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application ("App"). We respect your privacy and are transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect

Heart Scribed is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal to no personal information:

  • App Usage Data: The App may collect anonymous usage statistics (such as app crashes) to help us improve the App's performance. This data does not identify you personally.
  • Local Storage: All your saved verses, favorites, and app preferences are stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data.

How We Store Your Data

All data is stored locally on your device. This includes:

  • Your favorite Bible verses
  • Your memorization progress
  • App settings and preferences
  • Any notes or personalizations you create

We do not store, transmit, or have access to any of your personal data. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it (e.g., sharing a verse via your device's sharing features).

Third-Party Services

Heart Scribed does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data collection services. We do not share your data with any third parties.

Children's Privacy

Our App is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Since we do not collect personal information and all data is stored locally on the device, we comply with children's privacy protection requirements.

Data Security

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's built-in security features (such as device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID). We recommend keeping your device secure and regularly updating your iOS software.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete the App at any time, which will remove all locally stored data
  • You can manage your data through the App's settings
  • Since we don't collect personal information, there is no data for us to share or delete on our end

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App Store listing.

Apple App Store Privacy Information

In compliance with Apple App Store requirements, we declare:

  • Data Not Collected: We do not collect any user data, location data, or personal information.
  • Data Linked to You: None. All data remains on your device.
  • Data Used to Track You: None. We do not track users.
  • Data Shared with Third Parties: None. We do not share any data.
